How we work

A clear process at every step

Our process is built on communication, planning and managing risk early, so there are few surprises and the work ships on schedule.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We learn your business, users and constraints, analyse the specifications and agree on what success looks like.

  2. 02

    Plan

    We make architecture decisions, estimate the effort, map out the risks and build a roadmap you can follow in JIRA.

  3. 03

    Build

    We work in short iterations with regular demos and tested code. Designs are built from Figma to match exactly.

  4. 04

    Ship & support

    Automated deployments, monitoring, and ongoing maintenance for change requests and technical support.

Project-based
